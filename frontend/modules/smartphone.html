📱 What is a Smartphone?

A smartphone is a mobile device that allows you to make calls, send messages, browse the internet, use apps, take photos, watch videos, and communicate with others.

🔘 Main Parts of a Smartphone

  • Touch Screen
  • Power Button
  • Volume Buttons
  • Charging Port
  • Speaker
  • Microphone
  • Camera
  • SIM Card

👆 Basic Touch Gestures

  • Tap
  • Double Tap
  • Long Press
  • Swipe
  • Pinch to Zoom

🔋 Charging Tips

  • Use the original charger.
  • Avoid overcharging.
  • Keep battery between 20% and 80% whenever possible.
  • Do not use damaged charging cables.
