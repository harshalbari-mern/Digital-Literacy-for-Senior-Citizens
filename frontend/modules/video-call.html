Video Tutorial

Learn how to make video calls

🎥 What is Video Calling?

Video calling allows you to see and talk with your family, friends or colleagues in real time using your smartphone and an internet connection.

💬 WhatsApp Video Call

WhatsApp is one of the easiest apps for video calling.

  • Open WhatsApp.
  • Select a contact.
  • Tap the 📹 Video Call icon.
  • Wait for the other person to answer.

📹 Google Meet Video Call

Google Meet is commonly used for online classes and meetings.

  • Open Google Meet.
  • Tap Join with Code.
  • Enter the meeting code.
  • Tap Join.

🎙 Using Microphone & Camera

Control your microphone and camera during the meeting.

  • Tap the 🎤 icon to mute or unmute.
  • Tap the 📷 icon to turn the camera on or off.
  • Keep the camera steady.
  • Speak clearly.

📶 Internet Connection Tips

A good internet connection gives better video quality.

  • Use Wi-Fi whenever possible.
  • Stay near the Wi-Fi router.
  • Close unnecessary apps.
  • Ensure your phone is charged.

🔒 Safe Video Calling

  • Join meetings from trusted links only.
  • Do not share meeting codes publicly.
  • Keep your microphone muted when not speaking.
  • End the meeting after it is finished.

📝 Practice Activity

  • Make one WhatsApp video call.
  • Join a Google Meet meeting.
  • Mute and unmute your microphone.
  • Turn your camera ON and OFF.
