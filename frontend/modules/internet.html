🌐 What is the Internet?

The Internet is a worldwide network that allows people to search information, watch videos, send emails, use social media and communicate with others.

📶 Connecting to Wi-Fi

Wi-Fi gives your phone internet without using mobile data.

  • Open Settings.
  • Tap Wi-Fi.
  • Select your Wi-Fi network.
  • Enter the password.
  • Tap Connect.

📱 Using Mobile Data

If Wi-Fi is not available, you can use Mobile Data.

  • Open Settings.
  • Tap Mobile Network.
  • Turn Mobile Data ON.
  • Use data carefully.

🔍 Searching on Google

Google helps you find information quickly.

  • Open Google Chrome.
  • Type your question.
  • Tap Search.
  • Open a trusted website.

🛡 Safe Internet Browsing

Browse safely by following these simple rules.

  • Visit trusted websites only.
  • Never download unknown files.
  • Do not click suspicious advertisements.
  • Never share passwords online.

💡 Useful Internet Services

  • Google Search
  • YouTube
  • Google Maps
  • Online Banking
  • Government Services
