🔒 What is Cyber Safety?

Cyber Safety means protecting yourself while using the internet, mobile phones and online payment apps. Following safe online practices helps keep your money and personal information secure.

🔑 Create Strong Passwords

A strong password protects your accounts from hackers.

  • Use at least 8 characters.
  • Include letters, numbers and symbols.
  • Don't use your name or birth date.
  • Use a different password for different accounts.

📩 Beware of Fake SMS & Emails

Fraudsters often send fake messages pretending to be banks or government offices.

  • Do not click unknown links.
  • Check the sender carefully.
  • Delete suspicious messages immediately.
  • Never share personal information through SMS.

☎ Beware of Scam Calls

Scammers may call pretending to be from a bank, police or customer care.

  • Never share OTP.
  • Never share your ATM PIN.
  • Do not install unknown apps.
  • Disconnect suspicious calls immediately.

💳 Safe Online Payments

Always verify before making payments online.

  • Use trusted payment apps only.
  • Verify the receiver's name.
  • Never approve unknown payment requests.
  • Always check payment confirmation.

🛡 Privacy Tips

Protect your personal information online.

  • Keep your phone locked.
  • Don't share Aadhaar or PAN unnecessarily.
  • Review app permissions regularly.
  • Keep your phone updated.

📝 Practice Activity

  • Check if your phone has a screen lock.
  • Change one weak password.
  • Identify one fake-looking message.
  • Explain to a family member why OTP should never be shared.
