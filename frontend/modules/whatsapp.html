💬 What is WhatsApp?

WhatsApp is one of the world's most popular messaging applications. It allows users to send messages, photos, videos, voice messages, documents and make voice or video calls using the internet.

📝 Sending Messages

Sending a message is quick and easy.

  • Open WhatsApp.
  • Select a contact.
  • Type your message.
  • Tap the Send button.

🖼️ Sharing Photos & Videos

You can easily share your favourite memories with friends and family.

  • Open any chat.
  • Tap the 📎 Attachment icon.
  • Select Gallery or Camera.
  • Choose the photo or video.
  • Tap Send.

🎤 Sending Voice Messages

Voice messages are useful when typing is difficult. You can record your voice and send it instantly.

  • Open a chat.
  • Press and hold the 🎤 Microphone button.
  • Speak clearly.
  • Release the button to send the recording.

📹 WhatsApp Video Calling

WhatsApp allows you to make free video calls with your family and friends.

  • Open a contact.
  • Tap the 📹 Video Call icon.
  • Wait for the other person to answer.
  • Talk face-to-face.
  • Tap the End Call button to finish.

📂 Sharing Documents

You can also send PDF files, Word documents and other important files.

  • Open a chat.
  • Tap the 📎 Attachment icon.
  • Select Document.
  • Choose the file.
  • Tap Send.

🔒 WhatsApp Safety Tips

Follow these safety tips while using WhatsApp.

  • Never share your OTP.
  • Never share your bank details.
  • Do not click unknown links.
  • Block suspicious contacts.
  • Enable Two-Step Verification.

📝 Practice Activity

Complete these tasks for practice.

  • Send a "Hello" message.
  • Share one photo.
  • Send one voice message.
  • Make one WhatsApp video call.
