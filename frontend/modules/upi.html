💳 What is UPI?

UPI (Unified Payments Interface) is a digital payment system that allows you to send and receive money instantly using your smartphone. Popular UPI apps include Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and BHIM.

📲 Making Your First Payment

Follow these simple steps to pay using UPI.

  • Open your UPI app.
  • Select Scan & Pay.
  • Scan the QR Code.
  • Enter the payment amount.
  • Tap Pay.
  • Enter your UPI PIN.

💸 Sending Money

You can send money directly to your family and friends.

  • Open your UPI app.
  • Select the contact.
  • Enter the amount.
  • Tap Send.
  • Enter your UPI PIN.

💰 Receiving Money

Receiving money through UPI is quick and secure.

  • Open your UPI app.
  • Tap Receive Money.
  • Share your UPI ID or QR Code.
  • Wait for the sender to complete the payment.
  • Check the confirmation message.

📷 QR Code Payments

Most shops and stores accept QR Code payments.

  • Open your UPI app.
  • Tap Scan QR.
  • Scan the merchant's QR Code.
  • Enter the amount (if required).
  • Confirm using your UPI PIN.

🏦 Checking Bank Balance

You can check your bank balance directly from your UPI app.

  • Open your UPI app.
  • Select Check Balance.
  • Choose your bank account.
  • Enter your UPI PIN.
  • Your balance will be displayed.

🔒 UPI Safety Tips

Always follow these safety rules while making online payments.

  • Never share your UPI PIN.
  • Never share OTP with anyone.
  • Verify the receiver's name before paying.
  • Use only trusted UPI applications.
  • Do not click payment links sent by strangers.
  • If you receive a payment request unexpectedly, verify it before approving.

📝 Practice Activity

Practice these tasks with a family member.

  • Open your UPI application.
  • Scan a sample QR Code.
  • Check your bank balance.
  • Identify where the UPI PIN is entered.
  • Learn to recognize fake payment requests.
