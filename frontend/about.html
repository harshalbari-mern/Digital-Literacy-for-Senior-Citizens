About Our Project

Digital Literacy for Senior Citizens is an educational platform created to help elderly people learn how to use smartphones and digital services safely and confidently. This website provides easy-to-understand learning modules, practical video tutorials, interactive quizzes and certificates so that senior citizens can improve their digital skills.

Our Mission

📱 Smartphone Skills

Teach senior citizens how to use smartphones, calls, contacts, camera and settings.

💳 Digital Payments

Help users learn safe online payments, UPI transactions and banking apps.

🛡 Cyber Safety

Protect users from OTP fraud, fake calls and online scams.
